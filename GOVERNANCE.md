# Governance

ActionSeam is currently founder-maintained by **WM3 Digital**, with repository administration held by `duhenri9` during the experimental stage.

## Decision model

Maintainers are responsible for:

- release decisions;
- public contract changes;
- adapter support/maturity labels;
- security/disclosure coordination;
- provenance and license review;
- scope control.

Material architecture changes require an ADR. Evidence can overturn a prior technical assumption; changes should be recorded rather than silently rewritten into history.

## Maintainer expectations

Maintainers should:

- prefer reproducible evidence to reputation;
- keep limitations visible;
- avoid blanket claims about upstream projects;
- separate community launch from repository visibility;
- preserve the clean-room provenance boundary.

## Future governance

A broader maintainer model may be introduced after meaningful external contribution. Any repository ownership transfer or governance expansion should preserve ActionSeam's existing public history and licenses.
